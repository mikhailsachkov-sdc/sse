# Job Stories: Online Food Delivery App

**Application:** Online food delivery app (e.g., Wolt / Bolt Food)

**Format:** *When* [situation], *I want to* [motivation], *so I can* [expected outcome].

---

## Customer

1. **When** I'm sick at home and can't go out, **I want to** get soup and medicine delivered to my door, **so I can** recover without leaving bed.

2. **When** I have a food allergy, **I want to** filter out dishes that contain my allergen, **so I can** order safely without calling the restaurant to check every ingredient.

3. **When** my order is late and the app shows no progress, **I want to** know the real reason and a new arrival time, **so I can** decide whether to keep waiting or cancel and cook something myself.

4. **When** friends drop by unexpectedly in the evening, **I want to** order snacks and drinks that arrive within 30 minutes, **so I can** host them without running to the shop.

## Courier

5. **When** I'm delivering in heavy rain, **I want to** get orders with short routes close to where I am, **so I can** earn without riding across the city in bad weather.

6. **When** I arrive at a large apartment block, **I want to** see the exact entrance, floor and door code, **so I can** hand over the order quickly and move on to the next job.

## Restaurant

7. **When** the kitchen is overloaded during the lunch rush, **I want to** pause new orders or increase preparation time with one tap, **so I can** keep food quality up without disappointing customers.

8. **When** a popular dish runs out mid-evening, **I want to** mark it as unavailable instantly, **so I can** avoid cancellations and refunds for orders we cannot make.
