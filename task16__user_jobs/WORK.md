# User Jobs: Online Food Delivery

## User

A busy office worker who wants to get a hot, healthy meal delivered quickly to the office or home on busy workdays, without spending time on cooking or going out.

## Prompt Used

> I'm business analyst. I'm interested to analyze the user, who is interested in getting a hot, healthy meal delivered quickly to the office or home on busy workdays, without spending time on cooking or going out.
>
> 1. Describe please, what user is trying to do: tasks he tries to accomplish, problems wants to solve, needs he try to satisfy and so on.
> 2. Classify the jobs as physical, mental, or emotional.
> 3. Represent results in the table format: Job ! Job Type

---

## Result

The user is trying to get a hot, healthy, satisfying meal delivered to their office or home quickly and conveniently, without having to cook, travel, or interrupt a busy workday.

This is analysed using the **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)** framework, which distinguishes three types of jobs: **physical**, **mental** and **emotional**.

### 2. Summary by Job Type

| Job Type | Main User Goals |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | Get food, satisfy hunger, save time, avoid cooking and travelling, receive a hot meal quickly |
| **Mental** | Find healthy options, evaluate quality, compare alternatives, decide quickly, trust the delivery service |
| **Emotional** | Reduce stress, feel comfortable, maintain healthy habits, enjoy food, feel productive and in control |

### 3. Key Takeaway for Business Analysis

The user's core job is **not simply ordering food delivery**. It is:

> *"Help me get a hot, healthy, tasty meal whenever I am busy with work, so I can satisfy my hunger, maintain healthy eating habits, and save time and effort without interrupting my daily routine."*

This shifts the focus from the **product** (food delivery) to the **value the user wants to achieve**: convenient access to healthy meals with minimal time and effort.

**Priority value propositions for product development:**

1. **Speed** — fast and predictable delivery
2. **Health** — nutritious, fresh, hot meals with transparent ingredients
3. **Convenience** — easy ordering, minimal decision-making, delivery to the preferred location
