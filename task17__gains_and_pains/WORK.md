# Gains and Pains: Online Food Delivery

## User

A busy professional who wants a hot, healthy meal delivered quickly to the office or home without spending time cooking or going out.

## Prompt Used

> Return please to the list of user jobs. For each job detect:
>
> 1. Gains as a results thet user want to get.
> 2. Pains that irritate the user before, during and after trying to do his jobs.

---

## Result

Using the **Jobs-to-be-Done** and **Value Proposition Canvas** approach:

- **Jobs** — what the user wants to accomplish
- **Gains** — positive outcomes and benefits the user expects or desires
- **Pains** — problems, frustrations, risks and negative experiences before, during or after completing a job

Job numbers match the list in Task 16.

### 1. Physical Jobs

| # | User Job | Desired Gains | User Pains |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get a hot, freshly prepared meal delivered to the office or home | Enjoy a fresh, hot, tasty meal at a convenient location | Food arrives cold, spills during delivery, or looks different from the advertised meal |
| 2 | Satisfy hunger during a busy workday | Feel full, energised and satisfied; avoid hunger distractions | Hunger interrupts work; delivery takes too long; portions are too small |

| 5 | Receive food quickly, with minimal waiting time | Get food when needed, with predictable delivery times | Long or unpredictable delivery times, delayed orders, missed delivery windows |

### 4. Summary of the Most Important Gains and Pains

| Priority | Desired Gains | Major Pains |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Fast and predictable meal delivery | Long waiting times and delivery uncertainty |
| **Health** | Nutritious, balanced, healthy food | Difficulty finding genuinely healthy meals |
| **Convenience** | No cooking, shopping or travelling | Time and effort spent organising meals |
| **Food quality** | Hot, fresh, tasty meals | Cold, poor-quality or disappointing food |
| **Simplicity** | Quick ordering and easy decision-making | Complicated ordering and too many choices |
| **Reliability** | Confidence in delivery and service quality | Late, incorrect or cancelled orders |
| **Well-being** | Feel good physically and emotionally after eating | Guilt, stress, hunger and unhealthy eating habits |

### 5. Business Analysis: Key Insights

The user's main challenge is balancing three needs **without sacrificing any of them**:

1. **Health** — nutritious, balanced and tasty food
2. **Speed** — food received quickly, without long waiting times
3. **Convenience** — no cooking, shopping, travelling or time spent organising meals

**Core customer value proposition:**

> *"Get a delicious, hot, healthy meal delivered quickly to your preferred location, with minimal effort, so you can stay productive, save time, and feel good about what you eat."*

**From gains and pains to product requirements:**

| Customer Need | Potential Product Requirement |
|---|---|
| Fast delivery | Short and predictable delivery time |
| Healthy food | Nutritional information, calories and ingredient transparency |
| Hot food | Packaging that maintains food temperature |
| Convenient ordering | Simple ordering process with minimal steps |
| Easy decision-making | Clear menu categories and personalised recommendations |
| Reliable service | Accurate delivery estimates and order tracking |
| Good food quality | Consistent food preparation and quality control |
| Affordable meals | Transparent pricing without unexpected delivery fees |
