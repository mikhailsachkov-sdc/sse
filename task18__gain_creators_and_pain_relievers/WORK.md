# Gain Creators and Pain Relievers: Online Food Delivery

## User

A busy professional who wants a hot, healthy meal delivered quickly to the office or home without cooking or going out (same user as Tasks 16 and 17).

## Prompt Used

> Let's look to the online food delivery application.
>
> 1. Define please features of the application that are play the role of gain creators
> 2. Define please features of the application that are pain relievers.

---

## Result

The previously identified gains and pains (Task 17) are translated into features of the application, using the **Value Proposition Canvas** framework:

- **Gain Creators** — features that help users achieve desired benefits, positive experiences and additional value
- **Pain Relievers** — features that reduce frustrations, eliminate obstacles, minimise risks and solve existing problems

### 1. Gain Creators

| # | Application Feature | Gain Created for the User |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personalised meal recommendations | Quickly discover meals matching tastes, dietary preferences and nutritional goals |
| 2 | Healthy food categories | Find nutritious, balanced meals without browsing the entire menu |
| 3 | Nutritional information (calories, protein, fats, carbohydrates) | Make informed food choices and maintain healthy eating habits |
| 4 | Dietary filters (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, high-protein, etc.) | Find meals that match dietary requirements |
| 5 | Search by ingredients and meal type | Find exactly what is wanted quickly |
| 6 | Recommendations based on previous orders | Less decision-making time; relevant suggestions |
| 7 | Quick reorder / favourite meals | Repeat previous orders in a few clicks |
| 8 | Scheduled delivery | Plan meals in advance and receive them at a convenient time |
| 9 | Meal subscriptions or lunch plans | Regular meals without placing a new order every day |
| 10 | Loyalty programmes, bonuses and discounts | Save money and get additional value from repeated orders |
| 11 | Restaurant and meal ratings | Discover popular, well-reviewed meals and restaurants |
| 12 | Multiple restaurant and cuisine options | Variety and freedom of choice |
| 13 | Meal customisation | Adjust ingredients, portions and extras to personal preferences |
| 14 | Group ordering | Order meals together with colleagues or family |

### 2. Pain Relievers

| # | Application Feature | Pain Relieved for the User |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delivery time estimation before ordering | Less uncertainty about waiting time |
| 2 | Real-time order tracking | Less anxiety about delivery progress; easier time planning |
| 3 | Push notifications about order status | No need to constantly check the app |
| 4 | Delivery time slot selection | Avoids inconvenient delivery times and interruptions during meetings |
| 5 | Search and filters by delivery time | Avoids restaurants with long preparation or delivery times |
| 6 | Filters by price and delivery fee | Avoids unexpected expenses; helps stay within budget |
| 7 | Transparent pricing at checkout | Prevents surprises from hidden fees or extra charges |
| 8 | Secure and multiple payment methods | Fewer payment difficulties; more convenient ordering |
| 9 | Simple registration and checkout | Less time and effort to place an order |
| 10 | Saved delivery addresses | No need to re-enter office or home addresses |
| 11 | Clear ingredient and allergen information | Less uncertainty and risk of ordering unsuitable food |
| 12 | Restaurant availability and menu updates | Prevents ordering unavailable meals |
| 13 | Order modification or cancellation | Correct mistakes or react to unexpected changes |
| 14 | In-app customer support chat | Resolves delayed, incorrect or missing orders |
| 15 | Refund and compensation functionality | Less financial frustration when an order goes wrong |
| 16 | Delivery instructions (entrance, floor, access code) | Fewer communication problems; courier finds the customer easily |
| 17 | Food packaging information and quality standards | Lower risk of cold, damaged or spilled food |
| 18 | Reviews and complaints | Identify unreliable restaurants before ordering |

### 3. Summary: Gain Creators vs. Pain Relievers

The same application provides both types of value for the same customer need.

| Customer Need | Gain Creator | Pain Reliever |
|---|---|---|
| Get food quickly | Recommendations for meals available nearby | Delivery time estimates and real-time tracking |
| Eat healthy food | Healthy meal collections and nutritional recommendations | Ingredient, calorie and allergen information |
| Save time | Quick reorder and favourite meals | Simple registration and checkout |
| Avoid cooking and planning | Meal subscriptions and scheduled ordering | Saved preferences and delivery addresses |
| Receive quality food | Highly rated restaurants and recommended meals | Quality standards, packaging information, complaint handling |
| Save money | Loyalty points, bonuses and promotions | Transparent pricing and delivery fees |
| Reduce stress | Personalised meal suggestions | Order notifications and customer support |
| Get food at a convenient location | Multiple saved delivery addresses | Detailed delivery instructions and address validation |

### 4. Key Recommendations for Business Analysis

| Highest-Priority Gain Creators | Highest-Priority Pain Relievers |
|---|---|
| 1. Personalised healthy meal recommendations | 1. Accurate delivery time estimation |
| 2. Nutritional information and dietary filters | 2. Real-time order tracking |
| 3. Quick reorder and favourite meals | 3. Simple and fast checkout |
| 4. Scheduled meal delivery | 4. Transparent pricing |
| 5. Loyalty discounts and meal subscriptions | 5. Reliable customer support |
| | 6. Clear nutritional and allergen information |

**Key distinction:**

- **Gain Creators** make the application more **attractive** by providing additional value.
- **Pain Relievers** make the application **easier, safer and less frustrating** to use.

**Next step:** convert these features into a prioritised MVP feature list, separating essential features from those that can come in later releases.
